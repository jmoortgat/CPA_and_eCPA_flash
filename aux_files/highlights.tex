\noindent\textbf{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item CPA and eCPA EoS validated for CO$_2$+H$_2$O/brine from 0 to 425\,°C, 1 to 1500\,bar
  \item Newton--Raphson solvers with analytical Jacobians approach the speed of a cubic EoS
  \item 100\% flash convergence across $\sim$10,000 conditions; AARE 6.5\% vs.\ experiments
  \item Pre-computed solution table cuts flash cost by over two orders of magnitude
  \item Speed and robustness demonstrated in a prototype CO$_2$ reservoir simulator
\end{itemize}
